## 📋 Brand Identity

### Official Name
- **Primary Name**: TrvicERP
- **Full Name (English)**: Trvic Enterprise Resource Planning
- **Full Name (中文)**: 創域旅遊管理系統
- **Tagline (English)**: "AI-Powered Travel Intelligence"
- **Tagline (中文)**: "智能旅程，精準管理"

### Brand Naming Usage
✅ **Correct Usage**:
- TrvicERP (no space, capital T, capital ERP)
- Trvic (when referring to the company/product line)

❌ **Incorrect Usage**:
- TravelMaster, TravelMaster OS, travelmaster-os
- TrvicErp, Trvic-ERP, TRVICERP
- TravicERP, TrvicErp

## 🎨 Visual Identity

### Brand Colors

#### Primary Brand Color (Ocean Blue)
- **Purpose**: Brand identification, CTAs, important actions
- **Primary**: `#1F6FEB` (Ocean Blue)
- **Light**: `#EBF5FF` (Backgrounds, hover states)
- **Dark**: `#0D47A1` (Pressed states, dark mode)

#### Secondary Brand Color (Sky Cyan)
- **Purpose**: Accent color, highlights, active states
- **Primary**: `#38BDF8` (Sky Cyan)

#### Accent Color (Sunrise Orange)
- **Purpose**: Warnings, important notifications
- **Primary**: `#FF8C42` (Sunrise Orange)

### Functional Colors
- **Revenue/Success**: `#10B981` (Green)
- **Cost/Warning**: `#F59E0B` (Orange)
- **Customer**: `#8B5CF6` (Purple)
- **Operations**: `#3B82F6` (Blue)
- **Error**: `#EF4444` (Red)

## 🎯 Market Positioning

### Target Customers
1. **Mid-to-Large Travel Agencies** (Annual revenue > NT$50M)
2. **Corporate Welfare Committees** (500+ employees)
3. **Corporate Travel Management Departments**

### Value Proposition
"TrvicERP is Taiwan's first AI-native travel ERP system. From quotations to itinerary planning and cost analysis, we provide an all-in-one solution that increases travel business efficiency by 300% and reduces costs by 40%."

---

## 📐 Design Principles

1. **Clean & Modern** - Minimal clutter, generous whitespace
2. **Glassmorphism** - Semi-transparent surfaces, backdrop blur
3. **Travel-Inspired** - Sky and ocean color palette
4. **Accessible** - WCAG 2.1 AA standard compliance
